White Water Rafting Site Plan

Cathy Bisschoff

WDD 130-A1

Overview

Purpose

To build revenue for a whitewater rafting business by giving people everything they need to book a trip with confidence, and by showing those who want to go further that they can train to paddle and guide themselves. The site sells guided trips to first-timers and returning paddlers, fills course places for the school, and answers honestly what a day on the river actually involves, so that people arrive knowing what they have signed up for.

Audience

The site serves two audiences.

The first is trip customers: adults between 25 and 55, local weekenders and visiting tourists, comfortably able to spend on a day out but choosing between rafting and half a dozen other activities. They want an adventure they can talk about afterwards, not an ordeal. What they cannot find elsewhere is a straight answer to the questions that actually stop them booking – whether they need to swim well, how fit they need to be, what a grade 3 rapid really feels like, and what happens if they fall in. Most will find the site on a phone, following a link or searching while out, but many will move to a laptop before booking, because this is a real expense and they want to read the detail properly.

The second is students: paddlers from their late teens to their thirties who have already been on the water and want to do it properly. Some are working towards a guiding qualification and a job on the river; others simply want the skills. They want to know who is teaching them, how many hours they will spend paddling, what certification they come away with, and what it costs. They research carefully and compare providers, usually on a laptop, and will read far more detail than a trip customer ever would.
