About Me

A career in architecture and graphic design shapes how every page here is planned: structure first, then detail. Web development adds a new way to build what was once drawn. This page tells that story, from the drawing board to code written by hand.

Background

Architecture

An architect in high end residential design and hospitality interiors. Work began at SAOTA, a top international firm, and continued in Ireland and the United Kingdom.

Graphic Design

A graphic designer trained at BYU-Idaho, with more than 25 years of professional design experience. Work spans typography, editorial layout, illustration, photography and interaction design.

Web Development

A designer learning to code, working towards a BSc in Applied Software Development at BYU-Idaho. Web Fundamentals brought HTML and CSS written by hand, from wireframe to finished page.

Where I'm Heading

A BSc in Applied Software Development is the path forward, bringing a designer's eye to web and app development. Each project built along the way is collected on this portal.
